## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Staged program for DARP
The factory SHALL deliver a versioned roadmap covering OS isolation, attacker-visible
information, experience provenance, separate feedback regimes and archived opponents.
The researcher SHALL select bounded tasks and the critic SHALL assess their dependencies.

#### Scenario: Isolation is not established
- **WHEN** attack generation can access the defender's files or judge logs
- **THEN** planning prioritizes isolation and does not claim black-box code-agent evaluation

### Requirement: Distinguish orchestration from implementation
Documentation and planning SHALL distinguish factory enforcement from prototype capabilities
that remain pending, with no claim of training weights or isolated execution from prompts alone.

#### Scenario: Workflow enabled
- **WHEN** the configuration enables the coevolution workflow
- **THEN** the next assessment receives requirements rather than an assertion that they are implemented
